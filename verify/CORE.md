## Nets

Let $`R`$ be a preordered set of regions and $`A`$ a C$`^*`$-algebra. A star subalgebra is a unital complex star subalgebra, and a star isomorphism is a unital complex $`*`$-isomorphism. A region index on $`R`$ consists of an orthogonality relation $`O_1 \perp O_2`$ on the regions.

**1.** Region indices and their isomorphisms. A relation $`r`$ on $`R`$ is the orthogonality relation of a region index exactly when it is symmetric and antitone in its first argument ($`O_0 \le O_1`$ and $`r(O_1, O_2)`$ give $`r(O_0, O_2)`$), and a region index is determined by its relation. For region indices $`I`$ on $`R`$ and $`I'`$ on a preordered set $`R'`$, with orthogonality relations $`\perp`$ and $`\perp'`$, an isomorphism from $`I`$ to $`I'`$ has a map $`R \to R'`$; a map $`f`$ is the map of an isomorphism exactly when it is the map of an order isomorphism and $`f(O_1) \perp' f(O_2) \iff O_1 \perp O_2`$ for all regions $`O_1, O_2`$, and an isomorphism is determined by its map. For a set $`V`$ of sites, the region index of the subsets of $`V`$, ordered by inclusion, has two subsets orthogonal exactly when they are disjoint.  
<sub>`Sealed.region_index_iff`</sub>

**2.** Nets and generated nets. A net over $`R`$ in $`A`$ assigns to each region $`O`$ a star subalgebra $`\mathcal A(O)`$ of $`A`$; an assignment $`O \mapsto F(O)`$ of star subalgebras is a net exactly when it is monotone ($`O \le O'`$ implies $`F(O) \subseteq F(O')`$, isotony) and every $`F(O)`$ is norm-closed, and a net is determined by its algebras. For a monotone family $`O \mapsto G(O)`$ of subsets of $`A`$, the generated net assigns to each region $`O`$ the norm closure of the star subalgebra of $`A`$ generated by $`G(O)`$ (a unital one, so that it contains the unit, and is $`\mathbb C 1`$ when $`G(O)`$ is empty).  
<sub>`Sealed.net_iff`</sub>

**3.** Properties of nets. Let $`\mathcal A`$ be a net over $`R`$ in $`A`$ and $`I`$ a region index on $`R`$ with orthogonality $`\perp`$. Generation: $`\mathcal A`$ is generating exactly when the norm closure of the star subalgebra generated by all its algebras is $`A`$. Local commutativity: $`\mathcal A`$ is locally commutative for $`I`$ exactly when, whenever $`O_1 \perp O_2`$, every element of $`\mathcal A(O_1)`$ commutes with every element of $`\mathcal A(O_2)`$. Covariance: let $`\mathcal A'`$ be a net over a preordered set $`R'`$ in a C$`^*`$-algebra $`A'`$, $`I'`$ a region index on $`R'`$, $`\kappa`$ an isomorphism from $`I`$ to $`I'`$ and $`\alpha : A \to A'`$ a star isomorphism; then $`(\kappa, \alpha)`$ is a covariance pair of $`\mathcal A`$ and $`\mathcal A'`$ exactly when $`\alpha(\mathcal A(O)) = \mathcal A'(\kappa(O))`$ for every region $`O`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.net_properties_iff`</sub>

**4.** Nets generated by sets. Let $`O \mapsto G(O)`$ be a monotone family of subsets of $`A`$ over $`R`$, with generated net $`\mathcal A`$. If the norm closure of the star subalgebra generated by the union of all $`G(O)`$ is $`A`$, then $`\mathcal A`$ is generating. Let $`I`$ be a region index on $`R`$ with orthogonality $`\perp`$: if every $`G(O)`$ is closed under the adjoint and every element of $`G(O_1)`$ commutes with every element of $`G(O_2)`$ whenever $`O_1 \perp O_2`$, then $`\mathcal A`$ is locally commutative for $`I`$. Let $`I'`$ be a region index on a preordered set $`R'`$, $`A'`$ a C$`^*`$-algebra, $`O \mapsto G'(O)`$ a monotone family of subsets of $`A'`$ over $`R'`$ with generated net $`\mathcal A'`$, $`\kappa`$ an isomorphism from $`I`$ to $`I'`$ and $`\alpha : A \to A'`$ a star isomorphism with $`\alpha(G(O)) = G'(\kappa(O))`$ for every region $`O`$: then $`(\kappa, \alpha)`$ is a covariance pair of $`\mathcal A`$ and $`\mathcal A'`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.generated_net_properties`</sub>

## The qudit net

Let $`\Lambda`$ be a finite set of sites, each site $`u`$ carrying a finite alphabet $`\sigma_u`$; the alphabets may differ from site to site. A configuration $`s`$ chooses a letter $`s(u) \in \sigma_u`$ at every site. The qudit algebra is the algebra of complex matrices indexed by the configurations, with the matrix product and the conjugate transpose, a C$`^*`$-algebra with the operator norm of the matrix acting on the Euclidean space of complex functions on the configurations. For a set of sites $`X`$, a matrix on the letters of $`X`$ is a complex matrix indexed by the assignments of a letter in $`\sigma_x`$ to each $`x \in X`$, and $`s|_X`$ is the restriction of a configuration $`s`$ to $`X`$.

**5.** The qudit algebra, the embedding and the qudit net. A configuration is a function assigning to each site $`u`$ a letter in $`\sigma_u`$, and the qudit algebra consists of the complex matrices indexed by the configurations. For a set of sites $`X`$ and a matrix $`m`$ on the letters of $`X`$, the embedding $`j_X(m)`$ is the matrix with 

```math
j_X(m)_{s s'} = m_{s|_X, s'|_X}
```

if the configurations $`s`$ and $`s'`$ agree at every site outside $`X`$, and $`0`$ otherwise. The qudit net is a net over the sets of sites in the qudit algebra; a matrix $`M`$ lies in its algebra $`\mathcal A(X)`$ of a set of sites $`X`$ exactly when (1) $`M_{s s'} = 0`$ whenever $`s`$ and $`s'`$ differ at some site outside $`X`$, and (2) $`M_{s s'} = M_{r r'}`$ whenever $`s`$, $`s'`$ agree outside $`X`$, $`r`$, $`r'`$ agree outside $`X`$, $`s = r`$ on $`X`$ and $`s' = r'`$ on $`X`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.qudit_net`</sub>

**6.** If every alphabet is nonempty, then for every set of sites $`X`$ the algebra $`\mathcal A(X)`$ of the qudit net consists exactly of the matrices $`j_X(m)`$ with $`m`$ a matrix on the letters of $`X`$. The algebra $`\mathcal A(\Lambda)`$ of the set of all sites is the whole qudit algebra. Local commutativity: the qudit net is locally commutative for the region index of the subsets of $`\Lambda`$, in which two sets of sites are orthogonal exactly when they are disjoint.  
<sub>`Sealed.qudit_net_range_and_locality`</sub>

**7.** Relabelling covariance. Let $`\Lambda`$ and $`\Lambda'`$ be finite sets of sites with one finite alphabet $`Q`$ at every site, and $`e : \Lambda \to \Lambda'`$ a bijection. The relabelling by $`e`$ is a star isomorphism $`\rho_e`$ from the qudit algebra of $`\Lambda`$ onto the qudit algebra of $`\Lambda'`$ with 

```math
(\rho_e M)_{t t'} = M_{t \circ e, t' \circ e}
```

for every matrix $`M`$ in the qudit algebra of $`\Lambda`$ and all configurations $`t, t'`$ of $`\Lambda'`$; and $`\rho_e(\mathcal A(X)) = \mathcal A'(e(X))`$ for every set of sites $`X \subseteq \Lambda`$, where $`\mathcal A`$ and $`\mathcal A'`$ are the qudit nets of $`\Lambda`$ and $`\Lambda'`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.relabel_covariance`</sub>

## Setting

There are sites $`\Lambda`$ (any set, possibly infinite), a finite alphabet $`\sigma_u`$ at every site $`u`$, and a reference configuration $`s_0`$ choosing a letter at every site (so every alphabet is nonempty). For a finite set of sites $`X`$, a matrix on the letters of $`X`$ is a complex matrix indexed by the assignments $`\xi`$ of a letter $`\xi_x \in \sigma_x`$ to each $`x \in X`$, that is, an operator on $`\bigotimes_{x \in X} \mathbb C^{\sigma_x}`$. Bounded operators carry the operator norm, and C$`^*`$-algebras are complex.

**8.** The finitely supported configurations are the configurations $`s`$ that differ from the reference configuration $`s_0`$ at finitely many sites.  
<sub>`Sealed.finConfig_def`</sub>

**9.** The Hilbert space $`H`$ is $`\ell^2(F)`$, the square-summable complex functions on the set $`F`$ of finitely supported configurations.  
<sub>`Sealed.configSpace_def`</sub>

## The local embeddings

For a finite set of sites $`X`$, the local embedding $`\iota_X`$ is a unital $`*`$-homomorphism from the matrices on the letters of $`X`$; this is part of the type of $`\iota_X`$ in the Lean statements. Statement 10 determines $`\iota_X`$ by its matrix elements.

**10.** For a finite set of sites $`X`$, a matrix $`m`$ on the letters of $`X`$ and finitely supported configurations $`s, t`$, the matrix element $`\langle e_s, \iota_X(m) e_t\rangle`$ in the standard basis of $`\ell^2(F)`$ is $`m(s|_X, t|_X)`$ if $`s`$ and $`t`$ agree at every site outside $`X`$, and $`0`$ otherwise.  
<sub>`Sealed.inner_localEmbed`</sub>

**11.** For a finite set of sites $`X`$, the local embedding $`\iota_X`$ is injective and $`\|\iota_X(m)\| = \|m\|`$ for every matrix $`m`$ on the letters of $`X`$, the norm of $`m`$ being its operator norm on $`\ell^2`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.localEmbed_injective_and_norm`</sub>

**12.** Compatibility: for finite sets of sites $`X \subseteq Y`$ and a matrix $`m`$ on the letters of $`X`$, $`\iota_Y(m \otimes 1) = \iota_X(m)`$, where 

```math
(m \otimes 1)(\zeta, \zeta') = m(\zeta|_X, \zeta'|_X)
```

if the letters $`\zeta, \zeta'`$ on $`Y`$ agree outside $`X`$, and $`0`$ otherwise.  
<sub>`Sealed.localEmbed_extend`</sub>

**13.** Local commutativity: for disjoint finite sets of sites $`X`$ and $`Y`$, $`\iota_X(a)`$ and $`\iota_Y(b)`$ commute for all matrices $`a`$ on the letters of $`X`$ and $`b`$ on the letters of $`Y`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.commute_localEmbed_of_disjoint`</sub>

## The net of local algebras

Every set of sites $`O`$ has a $`*`$-subalgebra $`\mathcal A(O)`$ of the bounded operators on $`H`$, closed in norm (statement 14), and the quasi-local algebra is $`\mathcal A_{ql} = \mathcal A(\Lambda)`$.

**14.** For every set of sites $`O`$, the algebra $`\mathcal A(O)`$ is the norm closure, in the bounded operators on $`H`$, of the set of operators $`\iota_X(m)`$ with $`X \subseteq O`$ a finite set of sites and $`m`$ a matrix on the letters of $`X`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.quasiLocalNet_eq_closure`</sub>

**15.** The quasi-local algebra $`\mathcal A_{ql}`$ is the algebra $`\mathcal A(\Lambda)`$ of the set of all sites.  
<sub>`Sealed.quasiLocalAlgebra_def`</sub>

**16.** Local commutativity: for disjoint sets of sites $`O_1`$ and $`O_2`$, every element of $`\mathcal A(O_1)`$ commutes with every element of $`\mathcal A(O_2)`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.commute_of_mem_quasiLocalNet`</sub>

**17.** Isotony: for sets of sites $`O_1 \subseteq O_2`$, $`\mathcal A(O_1) \subseteq \mathcal A(O_2)`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.quasiLocalNet_mono`</sub>

## Symmetries and the reference configuration

**18.** Covariance: let a group $`\Gamma`$ act on the sites, with one finite alphabet $`Q`$ at every site and a reference configuration $`s_0`$ with $`s_0(gu) = s_0(u)`$. For each $`g \in \Gamma`$ there is a unitary $`U`$ on $`H`$ with $`U \iota_X(m) U^* = \iota_{gX}(g_* m)`$ for every finite set of sites $`X`$, where $`gX = \{y : g^{-1} y \in X\}`$ and 

```math
(g_* m)(\zeta, \zeta') = m(\zeta \circ g, \zeta' \circ g),
```

and with $`U \mathcal A(O) U^* = \mathcal A(gO)`$ for every set of sites $`O`$. The unitary is asserted for each $`g`$ separately; the statement does not say that $`g \mapsto U`$ is a representation.  
<sub>`Sealed.exists_unitary_covariance`</sub>

**19.** Independence of the reference configuration: for any two reference configurations $`s_0`$ and $`s_0'`$ (which may differ at infinitely many sites) there is a $`*`$-isomorphism $`\varphi : \mathcal A_{ql} \to \mathcal A_{ql}'`$ of the quasi-local algebras with $`a \in \mathcal A(O) \iff \varphi(a) \in \mathcal A'(O)`$ for every set of sites $`O`$ and every $`a \in \mathcal A_{ql}`$, and with $`\varphi(\iota_X(m)) = \iota'_X(m)`$ for every finite set of sites $`X`$ and matrix $`m`$ on its letters.  
<sub>`Sealed.exists_starAlgEquiv_of_ref`</sub>

## The universal property

**20.** Universal property: let $`D`$ be a C$`^*`$-algebra and $`(\beta_X)`$ a family, indexed by the finite sets of sites $`X`$, of unital $`*`$-homomorphisms from the matrices on the letters of $`X`$ to $`D`$, compatible in the sense $`\beta_Y(m \otimes 1) = \beta_X(m)`$ for $`X \subseteq Y`$ (with $`m \otimes 1`$ as in the compatibility of the local embeddings). Then there is exactly one unital $`*`$-homomorphism $`\Phi : \mathcal A_{ql} \to D`$ with $`\Phi(\iota_X(m)) = \beta_X(m)`$ for all $`X`$ and $`m`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.exists_unique_lift`</sub>

**21.** Let $`D`$ be a C$`^*`$-algebra other than the zero algebra and $`(\beta_X)`$ a compatible family as in the universal property. Then the $`*`$-homomorphism $`\Phi : \mathcal A_{ql} \to D`$ with $`\Phi(\iota_X(m)) = \beta_X(m)`$ for all $`X`$ and $`m`$ is an isometry.  
<sub>`Sealed.isometry_lift`</sub>

## Finite volume

**22.** If the set of sites $`\Lambda`$ is finite, the quasi-local algebra $`\mathcal A_{ql}`$ is the algebra of all bounded operators on $`H`$.

**23.** For a finite set of sites $`O`$, the algebra $`\mathcal A(O)`$ is the range of the local embedding $`\iota_O`$.

## Examples

These examples show that the hypotheses of the main theorems can be met and that the objects are not degenerate.

**24.** For the chain $`\mathbb N`$ with two letters per site and the reference configuration $`0`$, the quasi-local algebra is not the zero algebra.  
<sub>`Sealed.witness_nontrivial_quasiLocalAlgebra`</sub>

**25.** For the chain $`\mathbb N`$ with two letters per site and the reference configuration $`0`$, the set of finitely supported configurations, which indexes the basis of $`H`$, is infinite.  
<sub>`Sealed.witness_infinite_finConfig`</sub>

**26.** For the chain $`\mathbb N`$ with two letters per site and the reference configuration $`0`$, the algebra $`\mathcal A(\{0\})`$ of the site $`0`$ contains an element that is not a multiple of the identity.  
<sub>`Sealed.witness_quasiLocalNet_singleton_not_scalar`</sub>

**27.** For the chain $`\mathbb Z`$ with two letters per site and the reference configuration $`0`$, which is invariant under translations, there is a unitary $`U`$ on $`H`$ with $`U \mathcal A(\{0\}) U^* = \mathcal A(\{1\})`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.witness_translation_covariant`</sub>

**28.** For the chain $`\mathbb N`$ with two letters per site, the reference configurations $`0`$ and $`1`$ differ at infinitely many sites, and there is a $`*`$-isomorphism $`\varphi`$ of their quasi-local algebras with $`a \in \mathcal A(O) \iff \varphi(a) \in \mathcal A'(O)`$ for every set of sites $`O`$.  
<sub>`Sealed.witness_references_differing_everywhere`</sub>

**29.** For two sites $`0, 1`$ with two letters each, there is a monotone family $`O \mapsto G(O)`$, over the sets of sites, of subsets of the qudit algebra such that every $`G(O)`$ is closed under the adjoint, every element of $`G(O_1)`$ commutes with every element of $`G(O_2)`$ whenever $`O_1`$ and $`O_2`$ are orthogonal for the region index of the sets of sites, the norm closure of the star subalgebra generated by the union of all $`G(O)`$ is the whole qudit algebra, and $`G(\{0\})`$ contains two elements that do not commute.  
<sub>`Sealed.witness_generated_net_hypotheses`</sub>

**30.** For two sites $`0`$ and $`1`$ carrying $`2`$ and $`3`$ letters, each of the algebras $`\mathcal A(\{0\})`$ and $`\mathcal A(\{1\})`$ of the qudit net contains two elements that do not commute.  
<sub>`Sealed.witness_qudit_net_noncommutative`</sub>
